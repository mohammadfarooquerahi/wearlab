04 Product Catalog Management

WEARLAB is tailored for streetwear fashion drops. Garments typically have multiple sizes (S, M, L, XL), gallery image sliders, and inventory management.

Step-by-Step: Adding a New Streetwear Product

1
Create Product Entry Go to Products → Add New. Enter the product title (e.g. "Heavyweight Oversized Boxy Tee — Off Black") and write the fabric composition (e.g. 100% 280 GSM Combed Cotton).
2
Upload Primary Photo & Gallery Slider In the right sidebar, click Set product image for the primary front view. Then click Add product gallery images to select back view, side fit, and fabric close-up photos. The theme automatically arranges these into an interactive swipeable slider.
3
Configure Variable Sizes (S, M, L, XL) Under Product Data (below description), change dropdown from Simple product to Variable product. Click Attributes → Add: Name: Size | Values: S | M | L | XL. Check "Used for variations" and click Save attributes.
4
Set Prices & Stock for Each Size Click the Variations tab → Generate variations. 4 size cards will appear. Enter the price (e.g. 3490) and set stock status to In stock for each size.
5
Publish Product Select the category (e.g. T-Shirts) in the right panel and click the blue Publish button. The piece is immediately live on your store.

Sale Discounts & Badges

To put an item on sale, enter a Sale Price (PKR) alongside the Regular Price. The theme automatically displays a stylish SALE badge on the product card and strikes through the old price.

05 Order Management & Cash on Delivery (COD) Protocol

Cash on Delivery (COD) is the primary payment model for Pakistani e-commerce. To maintain high fulfillment rates and minimize Return-to-Origin (RTO) courier costs, follow this standardized order protocol:

1
New Order Received (Status: Processing) Customer places order on wearlab.store. Both customer and store receive instant order confirmation emails. Order appears under WooCommerce → Orders.
2
Customer Address & Size Verification Call / WhatsApp Before packing, contact the customer on WhatsApp or phone: "Assalam-o-Alaikum [Name], this is WEARLAB confirming your order #75 for [Product Name], Size [M], Total PKR 3,490 to [Delivery Address]..."
3
Packaging & Courier Portal Booking Inspect garment, pack in WEARLAB branded poly mailer. Log into your courier portal (PostEx, Trax, Leopard, or TCS), generate courier consignment note (CN), and affix airway bill.
4
Update Order Status to "Completed" In WP Admin, open the order and change status to Completed. Add courier tracking number into customer order notes so the customer is notified.
5
Doorstep Cash Collection Courier rider delivers parcel and collects exact cash in PKR. Courier deposits COD funds into your business bank account within 24–48 hours.

Order Status Definitions:

Status When to Use
Processing Default status when a COD order is placed. Awaiting phone verification and dispatch.
On Hold Use if customer phone is switched off or address details are incomplete.
Completed Parcel packaged, airway bill booked, and package handed over to courier.
Cancelled Customer requested cancellation prior to courier handoff.

09 Discount Coupons & Promotional Campaigns

Create discount codes for influencer drops, seasonal sales, or promotional campaigns:

  1. Go to Marketing → Coupons → Add coupon in the left sidebar.
  2. Enter the coupon code (e.g. DROP10 or FIRST500).
  3. Choose Discount type: Percentage discount (e.g. 10%) or Fixed cart discount (e.g. PKR 500).
  4. Under Usage restriction: (Optional) Set a minimum spend (e.g. PKR 5,000) or exclude sale items.
  5. Click Publish. Customers can apply this code at checkout for instant price deduction.
